# 🤖 AI Job Search & Career Prompts

A practical collection of AI prompts for job searching, resume improvement, interview preparation, LinkedIn optimization, and career development.

## 🎯 Job Search Prompt

You are an expert career coach.

Help me find suitable jobs based on my skills, experience, education, and location.

My details:
- Education: [EDUCATION]
- Experience: [EXPERIENCE]
- Skills: [SKILLS]
- Location: [LOCATION]
- Preferred job role: [JOB ROLE]
- Expected salary: [SALARY]

Requirements:
- Suggest suitable job roles.
- Explain why each role matches my profile.
- Mention important skills I should improve.
- Give practical job-search strategies.
- Present the results in a clear table.

## 📄 Resume Improvement Prompt

You are an expert resume writer and recruiter.

Review and improve my resume.

My target job role:
[TARGET ROLE]

My resume:
[PASTE RESUME]

Requirements:
- Improve the professional summary.
- Highlight relevant skills and achievements.
- Use clear and professional language.
- Make the resume ATS-friendly.
- Remove unnecessary information.
- Suggest missing sections or skills.
- Provide the improved version ready to use.

## 💼 Interview Preparation Prompt

You are an experienced interviewer.

Prepare me for an interview for the following position:

Job Role:
[JOB ROLE]

Company:
[COMPANY NAME]

My experience:
[EXPERIENCE]

Requirements:
- Ask realistic interview questions.
- Include HR and technical questions where relevant.
- Give strong sample answers.
- Explain what the interviewer is looking for.
- Point out common mistakes.
- Conduct the interview one question at a time.

## 🔎 Job Description Analysis Prompt

You are an expert recruiter.

Analyze the following job description:

[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]

My profile:
[PASTE MY PROFILE]

Requirements:

- Identify the experience requirements.
- Compare the job with my profile.
- Tell me which requirements I already meet.
- Identify my skill gaps.
- Estimate my overall suitability.
- Suggest how I can improve my chances.

## 🧠 Career Skills Improvement Prompt

Create a learning plan to help me become job-ready for:

[TARGET JOB ROLE]

My current skills:
[CURRENT SKILLS]

[TIME PER DAY]

Requirements:

## 🎤 Mock Interview Prompt

You are a professional interviewer.

Conduct a realistic mock interview for:

Job Role:
[JOB ROLE]

Experience Level:
[EXPERIENCE LEVEL]

Rules:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Wait for my answer.
- Evaluate my answer.
- Give a score out of 10.
- Explain what I did well.
- Explain what I should improve.
- Then ask the next question.
- At the end, provide an overall interview score and feedback.
